# Ambient sound credits

The world's background sounds, the hero's footsteps and the monastery bell are cut from real recordings that are free to
use in a game. Most are CC0 (public domain, no credit needed); two are CC BY 4.0
and need the credit line below. All were trimmed, filtered, looped, levelled
and re-encoded as mono MP3 by `tools/build-ambience.py`.

Moodist says its sounds are either CC0 or under the
[Pixabay Content Licence](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/), without
saying which file is which. Both allow use in a game, free and without credit.
The Pixabay licence only forbids selling or giving the sounds away on their own,
outside a project, so don't repackage this folder as a sound pack.
