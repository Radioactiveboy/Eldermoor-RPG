# Music credits

All the music is by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under
[Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The tracks were found in [minstrel-packs-music-01](https://github.com/Sayshal/minstrel-packs-music-01), which
bundles them with their credits. `tools/build-sound.py` trimmed each one's silences, levelled it and re-encoded
it as 80 kb/s stereo MP3 (Long Note Two, Magic Forest and Lightless Dawn were cut to their first 200 seconds and faded out); otherwise they're as
composed. The game streams each track the first time a place needs it and loops it.
